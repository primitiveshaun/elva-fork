My practice is based on continental philosophy of the twentieth century, heavily influenced by the works of psychoanalysts such as Sigmund Freud, Jacque Lacan, Melanie Klein, Winnicott and Bion.

**I provide various types of sessions:**
- Psychological assessments and treatments, also known as talking therapy.
- Psychological assessments and treatments, often referred to as talking therapy, involve the use of therapeutic two-way conversations and clinical assessments to understand, diagnose and address mental health concerns and improve emotional well-being.

**Clinical supervision to other clinicians**
- Clinical supervision involves an experienced clinician providing guidance, support, and oversight to other clinicians in order to enhance their professional development and improve the quality of patient care.

**Specialist assessments**
- Specialist assessments for neurodevelopmental conditions, such as autistic spectrum condition and ADHD, involve in-depth evaluations to diagnose and understand the specific cognitive and behavioral traits associated with these conditions.

Some conditions treated in the private clinic include:
– Interpersonal difficulties

– Depressive mood

– Anxiety

– Low self-esteem

– Stress

– Burnout

– Neurodevelopmental conditions such as autism and ADHD, in highly functioning adults

– Difficulties with adjusting to a significant and stressful event or life changes

– Adults who have suffered adversities during childhood, including abuse and neglect

A multilingual, clean, green, [11ty starter theme](https://www.11ty.dev/docs/starter/). [elva](https://github.com/scottsweb/elva) provides solid foundations for your next web project and a built in CMS ([Front Matter CMS](https://frontmatter.codes/)) for managing content. You are currently viewing the demo site which also contains a [little documentation]({{ "/" | locale_url }}) to help get you started. Head over to [GitHub to make feature requests, pull requests and report issues](https://github.com/scottsweb/elva/issues).

Features include:

### Performance things

* Optimised CSS, JS and HTML
* Responsive image shortcode that supports lazy loading and modern formats (avif, webp)
* Support for the [Photon CDN](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/photon/) (can be turned on via settings)

### Accessibility things

* [Multilingual](https://www.11ty.dev/docs/plugins/i18n/)
* Dark / light mode (see the toggle in the footer)
* Skip link and ARIA hints
